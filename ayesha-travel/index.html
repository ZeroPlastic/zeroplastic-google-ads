    About Ayesha Travel

    A personal starting point for your Sri Lankan journey

    Planning a trip to a new country can feel like a lot to hold at once. Ayesha Travel is a simple, personal place to begin.

    Tell Ayesha what draws you to Sri Lanka, how much time you have, and anything you need along the way, from airport transfers to accessibility requirements. Together, you can talk through suitable options and shape a journey that feels right for you.

    There is no fixed package to fit into. Whether you already have dates and a route in mind, or are simply curious about where to start, your enquiry is a conversation, not a commitment.

    • Shaped around you

      Your interests, your pace and your available time guide the conversation.

    • Room for questions

      Not sure how long to stay or where to go? That is a good place to start.

    • No pressure to book

      Sending an enquiry simply opens the discussion. Nothing is reserved or charged.

    How it works

    Three simple steps

    1. 01

      Choose your interests

      Browse the destinations and add the places that appeal to you. If you are unsure, choose “Help me choose”.

    2. 02

      Tell us about your trip

      Share your travel window, who is travelling and what kind of support would be helpful. Approximate details are fine.

    3. 03

      Discuss your travel plan with Ayesha

      Ayesha will reply to talk through suitable options with you and answer your questions.

    Please note: sending the form is an enquiry, not a confirmed booking. No payment is taken and nothing is reserved until you have discussed and agreed your plans with Ayesha.

        Privacy notice

        When you send a travel enquiry, we collect the details you choose to provide: your name and email address, and optionally your country of residence, WhatsApp number, travel dates, number of travellers, destinations, interests, preferred arrangements, accommodation preference, approximate budget and any message, including accessibility needs you decide to share.

        These details are sent by email to Ayesha and are used only to respond to your enquiry and discuss your travel plans with you. They are not sold or used for marketing. Our email delivery provider processes the message in order to deliver it. To ask about, correct or delete the information you have shared, please reply to any email from Ayesha.

        This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If you add destinations to your trip, that selection is stored only in your own browser so it is still there if you reload the page.
